# Equipment curriculum

Stage 1 runs in a serious home kitchen — domestic oven, fridge, freezer, counter — with no commercial bakery assumed. This section says what to buy, in what order, and what each tool lets you *control* or *measure*. Tier pages: [Essential](equipment/essential.md) · [Useful](equipment/useful.md) · [Professional](equipment/professional.md).

## Philosophy

1. **Buy instruments before gadgets.** A 1 g scale and a probe thermometer change your results more than any mixer. Most failures in Stage 1 trace back to unmeasured mass, unmeasured temperature or unmeasured time.
2. **Every tool must answer a question or control a variable.** If you cannot name the variable, you do not need the tool yet. The course introduces pH, °Brix and water activity only where the reading changes a decision.
3. **Know what the reading means.** An infrared thermometer reads a *surface*, not a core. A dial oven thermostat reports its own set point, not the air at your loaf. A pH strip is a range, not a number. Calibrate what you can ([X1-02](experiments/X1-02-calibration.md)) and record the uncertainty of what you cannot.
4. **Improvise first, upgrade on evidence.** Every Useful and Professional item has a manual equivalent that Stage 1 teaches. Buy the upgrade when the manual method has become the bottleneck in your notebook, not before.

## Suggested starter budget

Buy in this order; each group is enough to start the modules listed.

| Buy | Items | Band | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 1 g scale, precision scale, probe thermometer, oven thermometer, notebook, labels, timer | low | M00–M01 and every later formula |
| Week 2 | Bowls, straight-sided dough container, scrapers, cling film/containers, ruler | low | M02–M03 |
| Week 4 | Sheet pans, parchment, loaf pan, cooling rack | low | M03 |
| Week 6 | Covered cast-iron pot **or** baking stone/steel; rolling pin | medium | M04 onwards |
| Week 11 | Heavy-bottomed saucepans, whisk, silicone spatula, sieve | medium | M07–M09, M12–M13 |
| Week 14 | Two 20 cm round pans, tart ring, piping bags and tips | low | M10–M11, M13–M14 |
| When evidence says so | Stand mixer (brioche, buttercreams), refractometer (syrups), pH meter (levain), calipers (lamination) | medium–high | M05, M06, M13, M15 |

The Essential kit is low-to-medium overall; the pot or stone is the largest item. Stage 1 can be completed without a stand mixer.
