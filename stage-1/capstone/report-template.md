# Capstone report template

Copy this page into your lab notebook and fill in every section. Keep the headings: they map to the ten steps and to the rows of the [grading rubric](stage-1/capstone/README.md?id=grading-rubric). Tables can grow as long as you need. Record raw data during the bake and copy it here; do not reconstruct it afterwards. Guidance: [19.1](stage-1/module-19/lesson-01.md), [19.2](stage-1/module-19/lesson-02.md); worked example in the [capstone brief](stage-1/capstone/README.md).
