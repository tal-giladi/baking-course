# Professional Baking — Stage 1: Foundation

**20 modules · 66 lessons · 41 standardized recipes · 44 controlled experiments · 14 science units · capstone**

A professional baking and pastry curriculum for an adult learner who intends to go all the way
to **pastry R&D and original product development**. Stage 1 (about 3–6 months) makes you a
competent baker *and* gives you the scientific vocabulary and experimental habits that Stage 2
(advanced artisan and professional pastry) and Stage 3 (formulation and R&D) will build on.

The goal is not to make impressive pastries quickly. It is to build a baker who understands
what is happening, can reproduce results, can troubleshoot failures, can change variables
deliberately — and eventually can invent.

## How the course is built

Every module combines four dimensions:

| Dimension | What it means here |
|---|---|
| **Practical skill** | You make real products from standardized recipes with measurable endpoints — never "mix until ready". |
| **Baking science** | A [science track](stage-1/science/README.md) explains *why*, at exactly the depth needed to predict what a change will do. |
| **Experimental thinking** | Each module has controlled experiments in small batches: one variable, a hypothesis written before you start, an observation sheet. |
| **Professional workflow** | Baker's percentages, desired dough temperature, mise en place, scaling, documentation, food safety, production planning. |

The course teaches **systems, not recipes**: lean dough, enriched dough, short dough,
laminated dough, chemically leavened batters, egg foams, custards, emulsions, meringues,
choux. Each system is taught once, properly, and then reused.

## The three stages

| Stage | Duration | You become |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Professional Baking Foundation** (this course) | 3–6 months | A competent baker who reasons about baking systems |
| 2 — Advanced Artisan / Professional Pastry | 1–3 more years | A professional-level artisan and pastry practitioner |
| 3 — Baking/Pastry R&D and Product Development | 3–5+ years total | Someone who formulates, optimizes and invents |

Stage 1 is designed to be extended, not replaced: its concepts are organized into twelve
threads (T1–T12) and every later module plugs into one of them.
